### 👤 About Me

I'm a builder and operator based in **Singapore** 🇸🇬 — I run engineering as **CTO at [Aero Plus International](https://www.aeroplusgroup.com)** and serve as **Director at [The Winery](https://www.winery.com.sg)**. One exit behind me, and a stack of products in front.

- 🏗️ Building **B2B/B2C ordering & loyalty platforms** — invoicing, payments, and back-office integrations
- 🍷 Digitizing **wine distribution & hospitality** end to end
- ✈️ Shipping tech across an **aviation & holdings group**
- 🤖 Tinkering with **AI agents & LoRA optimization** on the side
- ⚡ I care about software that quietly does the operational heavy lifting
